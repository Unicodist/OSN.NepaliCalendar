Worksheet functions

Type =NEPALI. in a cell to see them all. Every argument takes a value, a cell or a range. Dates can be Excel dates or text like 2 Oct 2025, 16 Ashwin 2082 or २०८२-०६-१६.

=NEPALI.TOBS(A1)
BS date of the AD date in A1, like 2082-06-16.
=NEPALI.TOBS(A1:A100)
A whole range at once. The results spill into the cells below the formula.
=NEPALI.TOBS(A1, "D", "nepali")
The same date as १६ असोज २०८२.
=NEPALI.TOAD("2082-06-16")
AD date of a BS date. Format the cell as a date.
=NEPALI.DATE(2082, 6, 16)
AD date of a BS year, month and day.
=NEPALI.YEAR(A1), MONTH, DAY
Parts of the BS date of an AD date.
=NEPALI.FORMAT(B1, "dddd, d MMMM yyyy")
Reformats a BS date.
=NEPALI.TODAY()
Today's BS date.
=NEPALI.TONS(A1), =NEPALI.NSYEAR(A1)
Nepal Sambat date, like NS 1145 Kaula Thwa Dashami, and year.
=NEPALI.DAYSINMONTH(2082, 6), =NEPALI.MONTHNAME(6)
Month length and name.

Formats: d 2082-06-16, D 16 Ashwin 2082, F Bihibar, 16 Ashwin 2082, or custom with yyyy MM MMMM dd d dddd ddd.
